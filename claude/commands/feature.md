**`--self-review` flag (optional, short alias `-S`).** If you pass `--self-review` (long form) or `-S` (short form, same token/word-boundary detection rule as `-t`), you waive the Phase 4 Ruinor implementation review: DM presents you the raw diff verbatim and collects your verdict (REJECT / REVISE / ACCEPT / ACCEPT-WITH-RESERVATIONS) as a pure conduit, adding no assessment of its own. **`--self-review` implies `--trivial`** — passing `--self-review` alone is treated exactly as if you had also passed `--trivial`, so trivial tier is still established only through the explicit-assertion + Case A/Case B doubt rule above; DM's own silent self-classification never independently establishes the trivial tier this waiver depends on. Both `--trivial` and `--self-review` are constructive-pipeline-only flags: they are captured-but-ignored-with-warning in advisory or investigative sessions.
